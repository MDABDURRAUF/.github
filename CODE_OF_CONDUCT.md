# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The SupplyLens projects should be a place where anyone can ask a question, report a problem or propose a change without being made to feel unwelcome, whatever their background, experience or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient. Many participants are new to open source, to Python or to supply chain work.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Give credit for others' work.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and that a reason will be given.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space connected to the projects.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, use the "Report content" option on the comment or issue, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may block a participant from the projects for repeated or serious breaches.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
